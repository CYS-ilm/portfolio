About me

I am an aspiring Cybersecurity professional with a strong interest in Penetration Testing. I have completed the Certified Ethical Hacker (CEH) certification and am currently finishing my Cisco Certified Network Associate (CCNA) training. I have both theoretical and practical knowledge of networking, and I regularly work with Cisco Packet Tracer to build and simulate network environments.

My passion lies in ethical hacking and information security. I have hands-on experience through platforms like TryHackMe, where I’ve completed various labs to sharpen my skills in real-world scenarios. I also have a foundational understanding of Python, along with basic web development knowledge in HTML and CSS, which helps me better understand and analyze web-based vulnerabilities.

My goal is to become a skilled Penetration Tester, continuously learning and evolving with the fast-paced world of cybersecurity.
